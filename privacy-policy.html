Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you visit our website or choose to contact us.

Last reviewed: 3 November 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Cellartgen Lab Pvt. Ltd. ("Cellartgen", "we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with this website. It applies to information you choose to provide through the website and to basic technical information generated when the website is used.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you use a Contact or Enquiry form, we may receive the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, company name and message. If you apply for a career opportunity, we may receive your name, contact details, the position you apply for, your message and the resume or CV you upload.

Please provide only information relevant to your enquiry or application. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers or sensitive personal information unless we specifically request it through an appropriate secure process.

3. How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, communicate about business opportunities, review recruitment applications, operate the website, maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations. Access to enquiry and recruitment information should be limited to authorised personnel who need it for these purposes.

4. Storage and service providers

Website submissions may be stored in our application database. Resume files may be stored separately in the website's configured file storage. Our hosting, database and storage providers may process information on our behalf to provide these services. The exact provider and storage location depend on the deployment configuration.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

This policy does not claim that the public website uses analytics or advertising cookies. The administration area may use a strictly necessary session cookie to keep an authorised administrator signed in. Your browser settings can help you manage cookies, although blocking essential cookies may affect sign-in functionality.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not make submitted enquiry or applicant information publicly available. We may share it with authorised service providers supporting the website, or disclose it where required by law or necessary to protect our rights, users or systems.

7. Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, business records, recruitment processes, security or legal requirements.

8. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have submitted, request correction of inaccurate details, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and may retain information where permitted or required by law.

9. Third-party websites

Our website may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those third parties, and we encourage you to review their policies before providing information.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the contact details on our Contact page and mention “Privacy Request” in your message.

This page provides general website privacy information and is not legal advice. It should be reviewed against the website's actual hosting, storage, forms and business practices before publication.
