FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Injectable Manufacturing FAQs

What is pharmaceutical injectable manufacturing?

It refers to the development and production of pharmaceutical dosage forms intended for administration by injection, subject to applicable quality and regulatory requirements.

What types of injectable products are available?

Common categories include liquid solutions, suspensions and lyophilized products. Suitability depends on the formulation and intended product specifications.

Why is sterility important for injectable products?

Injectable products must meet applicable microbiological quality requirements. Sterility controls are essential to help protect product safety and quality.

What quality tests are relevant to injectables?

Depending on the product, evaluation may include assay, pH, sterility, bacterial endotoxins, particulate matter and stability testing.

Can injectable formulation development be customised?

Development requirements can be evaluated according to the API, dosage form, formulation objectives and applicable technical standards.
