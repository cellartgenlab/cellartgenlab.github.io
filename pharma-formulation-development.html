FORMULATION SCIENCE

Scientific Approach to Pharmaceutical Formulation Development

Pharmaceutical formulation development is the systematic process of designing a dosage form by selecting suitable active ingredients, excipients and manufacturing approaches. The objective is to develop a product that meets its intended quality, performance and stability requirements.

Cellartgen supports formulation development projects through product-specific technical evaluation, formulation planning and development activities according to the agreed project scope.

PRE-FORMULATION

Understanding API Properties Before Development

Preformulation evaluation helps identify important characteristics of an active pharmaceutical ingredient before finalizing a formulation strategy.

  • Physical appearance and material characteristics
  • Solubility and dissolution-related properties
  • Moisture sensitivity and hygroscopicity
  • Particle size and flow characteristics, where relevant
  • Thermal and chemical stability considerations
  • Compatibility with selected excipients
INGREDIENT ENGINEERING

API & Excipient Compatibility

Active pharmaceutical ingredients and excipients must be evaluated together to understand their suitability within the proposed formulation. Ingredient selection depends on the dosage form and target product profile.

Binders

Functional materials considered to support binding and structural integrity in suitable solid dosage forms.

Diluents & Fillers

Excipients used to achieve suitable formulation volume and processing characteristics.

Disintegrants

Ingredients evaluated for their role in dosage form breakup and product performance.

Stabilizers

Suitable materials considered to support formulation stability where required.

STABILITY DEVELOPMENT

Stability Studies & Shelf-Life Evaluation

Stability studies help determine how formulation characteristics change over time under specified environmental and storage conditions. The study design depends on the product category and applicable regulatory requirements.

  • Physical and chemical stability evaluation
  • Monitoring of relevant product quality attributes
  • Packaging compatibility assessment
  • Defined storage condition evaluation
  • Documentation of stability observations

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Pharmaceutical Formulation Development FAQs

What is pharmaceutical formulation development?

It is the process of designing and evaluating a pharmaceutical product by selecting suitable ingredients, excipients and dosage form characteristics.

What are preformulation studies?

Preformulation studies evaluate important physical and chemical properties of active ingredients before finalizing a formulation.

Why is excipient selection important?

Excipients influence processing, stability, physical properties and performance of pharmaceutical formulations.

Which dosage forms can be developed?

Development approaches may cover tablets, capsules, liquids, creams, ointments and other dosage forms depending on project scope.

Why are stability studies required?

Stability studies evaluate changes in product quality over time under defined storage conditions.

How can I start a formulation development project?

Share your product concept, API details, intended dosage form and technical requirements with Cellartgen to discuss project feasibility.
