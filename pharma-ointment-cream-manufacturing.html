TOPICAL FORMULATIONS

Pharmaceutical Topical Formulation Solutions

Topical pharmaceutical products are designed to deliver active ingredients through application to the skin or other appropriate body surfaces. Their performance depends on formulation composition, ingredient compatibility, physical characteristics and product stability.

Cellartgen supports pharmaceutical formulation development and manufacturing requirements for ointments, creams, gels and other topical dosage forms, based on the intended product specifications and project requirements.

FORMULATION DEVELOPMENT

Formulation Design & Development

Successful topical formulation development requires careful selection of active ingredients, excipients, bases and processing parameters. The formulation must be evaluated according to its intended use and target product profile.

Development considerations may include:

  • Active pharmaceutical ingredient compatibility
  • Selection of suitable ointment or cream base
  • Emulsifier and stabilizer selection
  • Viscosity and consistency optimization
  • Physical appearance and uniformity
  • Preservative compatibility, where applicable
  • Packaging compatibility assessment

PRODUCT STABILITY

Stability Studies & Packaging Compatibility

Stability studies help evaluate how a topical pharmaceutical formulation behaves under defined storage conditions over time. These studies support product quality assessment and shelf-life evaluation.

Packaging compatibility is also important because the container and closure system may influence product quality during storage and use.

  • Physical stability assessment
  • Appearance and consistency monitoring
  • Relevant chemical and microbiological evaluations
  • Container and closure compatibility
  • Storage condition evaluation

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Ointment & Cream Manufacturing FAQs

What is pharmaceutical ointment manufacturing?

It involves developing and producing semi-solid pharmaceutical formulations using suitable ingredients and bases according to product specifications.

What is the difference between ointments and creams?

Ointments are generally formulated using an ointment base, while creams are typically emulsion-based semi-solid preparations.

Can Cellartgen support cream formulation development?

Cellartgen can discuss formulation development requirements based on the product concept, technical scope and project feasibility.

What quality tests are relevant for topical products?

Depending on the formulation, evaluations may include appearance, homogeneity, pH, viscosity, microbial quality and stability.

Which packaging options are used for creams and ointments?

Options may include collapsible tubes, jars and suitable plastic containers depending on the formulation and packaging requirements.

How can I discuss a manufacturing project?
