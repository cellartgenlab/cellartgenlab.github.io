LIQUID DOSAGE FORMS

Types of Pharmaceutical Liquid Products

Different liquid dosage forms require different formulation strategies and manufacturing controls.

Pharmaceutical Syrups

Syrups are liquid preparations that may contain dissolved active ingredients in a suitable vehicle. Formulation development considers sweetness, viscosity, taste, stability and product specifications.

Oral Solutions

Oral solutions contain active ingredients dissolved in a suitable solvent or vehicle. Formulation design considers solubility, pH, compatibility and stability.

Oral Suspensions

Suspensions contain finely dispersed solid particles within a liquid medium. Important characteristics include sedimentation, redispersibility and dose uniformity after shaking.

Oral Emulsions

Emulsions are systems containing two immiscible liquid phases. Appropriate emulsifiers and processing methods are selected according to product requirements.

Liquid Drops

Liquid drop formulations require suitable attention to concentration, dispensing accuracy, packaging compatibility and product stability.

Specialty Liquid Formulations

Certain products require specific viscosity, flavour, preservation, solubility or delivery characteristics based on their intended use.

FORMULATION SCIENCE

Liquid Formulation Development

Liquid formulation development starts with a detailed assessment of the active ingredient and its behaviour in the proposed liquid system. Each ingredient must be selected according to compatibility and product performance requirements.

1. Active Ingredient Evaluation

Evaluation of solubility, chemical stability, physical characteristics and compatibility with the proposed formulation ingredients.

2. Vehicle and Solvent Selection

Selection of an appropriate aqueous or other suitable vehicle according to the active ingredient and intended dosage form.

3. Excipient Selection

Evaluation of suitable sweeteners, preservatives, buffers, viscosity modifiers, stabilizers, flavours and other formulation ingredients where appropriate.

4. pH and Viscosity Optimization

Assessment of pH and viscosity to support the required product characteristics and stability.

5. Prototype Development

Preparation and evaluation of suitable formulation prototypes before establishing the proposed manufacturing process.

MANUFACTURING WORKFLOW

Pharmaceutical Liquid Manufacturing Process

A liquid manufacturing process is designed according to the formulation, equipment, batch size and product specifications. A typical workflow may include:

  • Raw material receipt and quality approval
  • Dispensing and weighing of ingredients
  • Preparation of the liquid vehicle
  • Dissolution or dispersion of ingredients
  • Controlled mixing and homogenization where required
  • pH and viscosity adjustment as per formulation
  • Filtration or other suitable processing where applicable
  • In-process quality evaluation
  • Filling and packaging
  • Finished product testing and batch review

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Syrup & Liquid Manufacturing FAQs

What is pharmaceutical liquid manufacturing?

It is the process of developing and manufacturing liquid dosage forms such as syrups, solutions, suspensions and emulsions using suitable ingredients, equipment and quality controls.

What is the difference between a syrup and a suspension?

A syrup is generally a liquid preparation containing dissolved ingredients, while a suspension contains dispersed solid particles in a liquid medium.

Why is pH important in liquid formulations?

pH can influence ingredient stability, solubility, compatibility and other formulation characteristics.

Why is viscosity testing performed?

Viscosity testing helps evaluate flow behaviour, consistency and product characteristics where applicable.

How is liquid product packaging selected?

Packaging is selected after considering formulation compatibility, product stability, storage and distribution requirements.

What information is required for a manufacturing enquiry?

Product type, formulation details, target pack size, estimated quantity and project requirements help initiate a manufacturing discussion.
